What is the sender of record?

{{ sender_help }}

Organisation
Who writes: “We write as {{ values.org_name or org_default }} …”, and the signature's last line. Default: {{ org_default }}.
On behalf of
The victim the letters are written for (required): “… on behalf of {{ values.on_behalf_of or '…' }}”. Never put in a mail header.
Signatory and title
Who signs, above the organisation line. Default: {{ signatory_default }} (the investigations desk).
Contact e-mail
Printed in the signature, and offered as “Cc the sender of record” (ticked) when an e-mail is sent. Empty: the case mailbox, which needs no Cc (its Sent Items keeps the copy).
Authority instrument
What the victim signed: “acting under the victim's {{ values.instrument or instrument_default }} (attached)”. Default: {{ instrument_default }}; only say Power of Attorney when that is literally what is on file.
Signature block
Optional: replaces the composed signature lines, one line each.
Authority documents
Offered (pre-ticked) as attachments when an e-mail is prepared; TLP:RED / TLP:AMBER items can never be attached.

The e-mail's From is the configured case mailbox and its display name. A letter written before the profile was set up (or changed) is flagged in the e-mail editor, with a one-click Use the sender profile's signature; letters already sent or imported are never changed.
