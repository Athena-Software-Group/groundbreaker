How the moves are ranked

score = value × urgency × likelihood ÷ effort. Value: the USD at stake — the balance an issuer could still freeze (read on-chain), else the amount traced to the target (an upper bound until a reading or a reply confirms it), or what a person confirmed held. Urgency (×1–4): a clock close to its end (a provider deadline, a hold's expiry, an overdue reply), funds observed moving (a movement alert after the trace, an issuer lifting its freeze), a recent arrival, nobody asked yet. Likelihood: what the target can do (an issuer's blacklist, an exchange account, records only) × the provider's playbook for a letter from us (a voluntary hold, LE process only, no playbook) × the route (the case's LE authority can ask directly, or cross-border) × how far the request got (a hold in place, LE filed, an order). Effort: the kind of move, plus the evidence still to obtain. Hover “why this rank” on a move for its own urgency, likelihood, effort and score.

The figures above. In play: the moves' values, each traced dollar once (one service's funds that came through another count once), capped at the traced loss. Owner unknown: the wallets holding {{ '$' ~ '{:,.0f}'.format(t.unknown_owner.threshold_usd) if t.unknown_owner else '$1,000' }} or more (the larger of $1,000 and 1% of the loss) whose owner no source names — the "Identify the owner" move and the Overview tile use the same figure. Overdue: the replies Block status counts as overdue. The Overview's tiles show these same figures.

Advisory: Aegis's own ranking from rules, no AI. A hold, freeze or return counts only once a person confirms it; a person drafts, reviews and sends every letter. LE-only channels are never offered to us: those moves brief the case officer with the pack. Details: docs/recovery-plan.md.
